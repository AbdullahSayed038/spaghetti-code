Sep 16, 2026 v3.0.0 Release

Nimbus 3.0: answers across your whole workspace

Ask a question in plain words and Nimbus answers from your own notes, with links to every source it used. It respects page permissions, so people only get answers from notes they can already read.

  • Answers cite the exact paragraphs they came from
  • Works across imported Notion and Confluence pages
  • Admins can turn answers off per workspace
  • Available on Team and Business plans today

Sep 02, 2026 v2.14.0 Feature

Stale-page alerts

Docs quietly rot. Nimbus now tracks when a page was last reviewed and nudges its owner after 90 days, so new teammates stop trusting outdated instructions.

  • Owners get a weekly digest of pages that need a look
  • Readers see a gentle banner on stale pages
  • Set a custom review window per space

Aug 19, 2026 v2.13.0 Improvement

Offline mode for desktop

Keep writing on a plane or in a tunnel. Changes are stored locally and merged automatically the moment you are back online, even if a teammate edited the same page.

  • Conflict-free merging for simultaneous edits
  • Your 200 most recent pages are always available offline
  • A clear sync indicator in the sidebar
Aug 05, 2026 v2.12.0 Feature

Team templates

Save any page as a template and share it with your workspace. Meeting notes, incident reports and onboarding checklists now start from the same shape every time.

  • Template gallery with 40 starter templates
  • Variables like {date} and {author} fill in automatically
  • Pin favourite templates to the new-page menu
Jul 22, 2026 v2.11.2 Improvement

Search is 3x faster

We rebuilt the search index from scratch. Median search time dropped from 120ms to 38ms, and results inside images and PDFs now appear instantly.

  • New index built on incremental updates
  • Typo-tolerant matching for names and code
  • Search inside attachments without waiting

Jul 09, 2026 v2.11.1 Fix

Guest access fixes

A handful of fixes for guests: expiring links now expire on time in every timezone, and guests can no longer see the names of private spaces in search suggestions.

  • Expiry dates respect the workspace timezone
  • Private space names hidden from guest suggestions
  • Clearer error when a guest link has expired
